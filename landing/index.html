❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Cohabio find compatible people?
We use a combination of verified identities and detailed lifestyle preferences to find people who match your vibe. Instead of just showing listings, we focus on the people first, ensuring you connect with roommates who share your living style.
How is compatibility calculated?
Our algorithm calculates a deterministic percentage based on several weighted factors: Budget Overlap (25%), Cleanliness Rating (20%), Sleep/Work Schedule (20%), Habits/Lifestyle (15%), Food Preferences (10%), and Shared Interests/Hobbies (10%).
Can I choose my preferences?
Absolutely. You have full control over your preferences. You can adjust your budget range, set your cleanliness standards, select your sleep routine, and specify dietary choices. The app will only recommend profiles that align with your settings.
Is my personal information safe?
Security is our top priority. We use industry-standard encryption and do not share your private data with third parties. Profiles undergo a 2-level verification process (Email OTP & Identity Verification) to ensure a safe and trustworthy community.
Who can use Cohabio?
Cohabio is primarily designed for students, fresh graduates, and young professionals who are relocating to new cities and looking for compatible roommates and verified housing options.
How does the waitlist work?
By joining the waitlist, you reserve early access to the Cohabio mobile app before its official public launch. You'll receive updates, exclusive invites, and priority placement in our verified communities.
